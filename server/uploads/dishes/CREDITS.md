# Dish photo credits

All 63 images come from **Wikimedia Commons** and are used under the licence
shown against each one. Attribution is a condition of these licences, not a
courtesy — keep this file with the images if you reuse them.

Every photo was **checked by eye** against the dish it represents. Automated
search is unreliable for this: first-pass results included a shopping mall for
"crispy corn", a *restaurant storefront* for pepperoni pizza, a chicken curry for
a vegan dish, and a beef bowl for a vegetarian soup. Anything wrong was replaced
and re-checked.

## Known imperfections

- **Chicken Caesar Salad** — Caesar salad, but no chicken visible in the photo.
- **Goan Fish Curry** — A Goan fish thali rather than a close-up of the curry alone.
- **Malai Kofta** — Titled malai kofta on Commons; a dim restaurant snapshot rather than a clean plate shot.
- **Penne Arrabbiata** — Correct dish; the photo has a parmesan garnish although the menu marks it vegan.
- **Quinoa Avocado Bowl** — Shows avocado but not quinoa — no Commons photo matches both.
